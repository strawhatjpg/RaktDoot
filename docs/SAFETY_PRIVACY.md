# Safety and privacy

## Safety

- Donors are directed only to licensed blood centres. No person-to-person collection.
- AI never makes clinical decisions (urgency, fitness, compatibility, substitution).
- Eligibility reminders are provisional; the blood centre's screening decides.
- Stock is labelled reported / confirmed / reserved with source time; simulated data is labelled.
- For emergencies, the family is always told to follow the treating hospital's guidance.

## Privacy (designed with India's DPDP Act, 2023 in mind; not legal advice)

- Purpose-specific consent asked in-chat / in-app; withdrawable any time ("STOP", app toggles).
- Location stored at ~1 km precision; updated only when the donor chooses.
- Donor numbers never shown to requesters without CONTACT_SHARING consent for that request.
- Data minimisation: no Aadhaar, no medical history beyond last donation date.
- "DELETE MY DATA" removes the profile; audit entries keep only non-identifying references.

## Abuse protection

- Verification before private donor outreach.
- Rate limits per phone number; duplicate request detection (same hospital + group + time window).
- Report / block from donor messages.
- No payment solicitation features.
